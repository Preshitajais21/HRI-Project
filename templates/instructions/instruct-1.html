Instructions – Part 1: Interactive Robot

In this first part, you will supervise a hospital inventory robot. The robot will ask you what items to collect, and you will decide how much it should restock.

Step 1
Overall screen layout

When the experiment starts, you will see a screen similar to the one below. It has four main areas: Inventory (left), Current Task (middle), Robot / Questions (right), and the Robot Video (bottom).

Example of the full experiment screen.
Step 2
Inventory panel (left)

The Inventory panel shows item names (for example, Gloves, Syringes, Masks) and their current counts.

  • Click an item to send 1 unit toward the current task.
  • You can click the same item multiple times to send more units.

These clicks represent you telling the robot which items to send to the hospital.

Close-up of the Inventory panel.
Step 3
Current Task panel (middle)

The Current Task panel tells you what the hospital currently needs (for example, “2 Gloves, 3 Masks”).

  • Use the Inventory panel to send items toward this task.
  • When you are satisfied with what you have sent, click “Submit Task” to finish that task.

After you submit, the next task will appear after a short delay.

Close-up of the Current Task panel.
Step 4
Robot / Questions panel (right)

The Robot / Questions panel shows messages from the robot. In this interactive part, the robot will sometimes ask: “How many of each item should I collect?”

  • Enter how many units of each item the robot should collect (for example, 3 Cotton Rolls, 2 Syringes, 1 IV Bag).
  • Start the collection, and the robot will restock those items.

Try to give helpful amounts that keep the inventory at a reasonable level.

Example of a robot question during the interactive part.
Step 5
Robot video (bottom)

At the bottom of the screen there is a video of the robot. The video plays while the robot is restocking items.

You do not need to click on the video, but it gives you a visual sense that the robot is “working” while items are being collected. During this part, focus mainly on the Inventory, Current Task, and Robot / Questions panels.

Screenshot of the robot video area.

This part will last about 2.5 minutes. When you are ready, click the button below to begin.

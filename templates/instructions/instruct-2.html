Instructions – Part 2: Silent Robot

In this second part, you will supervise the same hospital inventory robot, but now the robot will restock items on its own without asking you what to collect.

Your job is still to complete the hospital tasks using the Inventory and Current Task panels, while the robot silently keeps the supplies topped up.

Overall screen layout

The main screen looks very similar to the previous part: Inventory (left), Current Task (middle), Robot / Questions (right), and the Robot Video (bottom).

Full experiment screen
Inventory panel (left)

The Inventory panel is the same as before. It shows the available items and their current counts.

  • Click an item name to send 1 unit toward the current task.
  • You can click the same item multiple times to send more units.

These clicks represent you telling the robot which items to send out to the hospital.

Inventory panel
Current Task panel (middle)

The Current Task panel again shows what the hospital currently needs (for example, “2 Gloves, 3 Masks”).

  • Use the Inventory panel to send items toward this task.
  • When you are satisfied with what you have sent, click “Submit Task” to finish that task.

The next task will appear after a short delay, just as in the first part.

Current task example
Silent robot behavior (right panel)

In this part, the robot sometimes restocks items automatically. When this happens, you will see a short message on the right panel such as: “(Silent) Restocking supplies…”

  • The robot chooses what to collect; you do not enter numbers for it.
  • While the robot is restocking, the inventory counts will increase automatically.
  • You should continue focusing on completing the Current Task as best you can.

Compare this experience with the first part, where you actively chose the restocking amounts.

Example of a silent restock message
Robot video (bottom)

When the robot is silently restocking items, the robot video plays, just as before. This represents the robot moving and collecting supplies.

You do not need to click on the video – it is there to give you a sense of the robot working in the background while you manage the tasks.
